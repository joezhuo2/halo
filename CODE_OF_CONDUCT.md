# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in Halo, in issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, agrees to make it a welcoming and harassment-free project for all
people, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes, and learn from them.
- Keep the focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or
seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer (joezhuo2 on GitHub) privately.
Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
